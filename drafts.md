## Winner: Draft #17 — 25/25 PERFECT

"I bought the dip. Then the dip bought a shovel and kept digging." (63 chars)

Why it wins against the judge brief:
- Setup + turn: "I bought the dip" is the most recognisable trading move;
  the turn personifies the dip itself buying a shovel — surprise, visual, instant.
- Truth: every trader has watched a dip keep dipping; the detail is earned,
  not generic.
- Tight: 63 chars, no throat-clearing, punchline ("kept digging") is the last
  two words.
- Repeatable: a trader would say this to a friend after a red day; it holds
  up on a second read because the image (dip with a shovel) keeps working.

Runner-ups (#6, #19, #21, #22 at 22/25) lose on the laugh criterion: their
turns are clever but expected, while #17's turn is a genuine surprise.
